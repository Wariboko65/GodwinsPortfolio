# Portfolio motion film: three storylines

One thing to remember: **one person, two careers: he builds software and he builds the physical world.**
Every fact comes from `src/data/*.ts`. Music only (no voice), ~30 s, 16:9 and 9:16.

## A. Two paths, one shape (recommended)
Device: match cuts. Form: split screen to one.
- **What you see:** a booking card from the JOPAD app becomes the 450 × 450 mm plinth outline on an NDDC site photo. A UI grid becomes a bar chart of hostel waste. Every software object turns into an engineering object, then the two halves of the screen close into one name.
- **First 2 s:** the name lands on a frame split down the middle: dark code world on the left, paper-and-site world on the right. A bass hit lands on the split.
- **Beats:** hook (name splits) → software proof (JOPAD booking flow acts: slot → server check → calendar invite → email) → 14 Frontend Mentor solutions in a 3D gallery tunnel → **match cut**: card becomes plinth → NDDC field inspection → 150.4 kg number lands, bar chart builds → halves close → end card + URL.
- **Look:** the site's own two grounds (dark /software, paper /engineering), the lime accent rationed to the payoff and the URL.
- **Sound:** 120 BPM deep cinematic electronic with sub bass. The drop lands on the card→plinth match cut, a riser goes into 150.4 kg, and a bass impact hits on the name.

## B. One continuous flight
Device: the object is the explanation. Form: one continuous take.
- **What you see:** the camera never cuts. It dives through a laptop screen into the JOPAD flow, out through a site photo onto the NDDC ground, then pulls up into the waste chart and out to the name. It's a drone shot through one long world.
- **First 2 s:** an extreme close-up of a code cursor blinking, already moving toward the camera.
- **Beats:** dive into code → booking flow → fly through the 14 solutions → dive through a site photo → plinth draws → waste chart rises → pull back to the name.
- **Look:** depth of field, one warm key light, the two grounds blending as the camera travels.
- **Sound:** a slow-building bass swell with no hard drops until the final pull-back.

## C. Verbs on the beat
Device: type carries the argument. Form: cut on the beat.
- **What you see:** giant kinetic verbs change every half beat over full-colour fields, each flashing its real proof for under a second: "BOOKS" (JOPAD calendar invite), "SHIPS" (14 solutions), "INSPECTS" (plinth), "WEIGHS" (150.4 kg).
- **First 2 s:** "BUILDS" fills the frame, then the word changes to "CODE" and then to "CONCRETE" on the next two kicks.
- **Beats:** verb montage (software) → bass drop → verb montage (engineering) → the name.
- **Look:** flat colour fields from the site palette, tight heavy type, hard cuts.
- **Sound:** hard-hitting 120 BPM with heavy sub bass. Every cut is on a kick and the drop comes halfway.
